About EROL

rooted in imphal. serving all of manipur.

A language service built on one belief: everyone in Manipur deserves information in the language they understand best.

01 Our story

one standard for every language.

EROL Manipuri Language Service was founded in Imphal in 2025. We bring together native-speaker linguists from communities across the state to translate, transcribe and voice content in Meiteilon and the tribal languages of Manipur.

Whichever language you need, it gets the same care: faithful to meaning, clear to read, and respectful of the people who speak it.

  • Every community, equallyWe serve all of Manipur's communities and keep our work neutral.
  • Faithful to meaningNames, numbers and nuance carried over accurately.
  • Clear to readWriting that sounds natural to the people you want to reach.
