FAQ Questions

good to know.

Which languages do you work in?

Meiteilon (Manipuri) and the languages of Manipur's hill communities, 33 in all, plus English and Hindi. If you need a language that isn't listed, ask us.

How much does a translation cost?

It depends on the language pair, the length and subject of the content, and your deadline. We send a fixed quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How long will my project take?

We agree a delivery date with you when we send the quote. Tell us if you have a firm deadline. Urgent work is often possible.

Can you translate between two languages of Manipur?

Yes, for example from Meiteilon into Tangkhul or Paite. Many public-information projects need several languages at once, and we can coordinate them together.

Will my documents be kept confidential?

Yes. Files are shared only with the linguists working on your project, and we're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement.

Which file formats do you accept?

Word, PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text, audio and video files, and subtitle formats such as SRT and VTT. If you use another format, let us know.
