What we do

Services that help you be understood

From a one-page notice to a full documentary, every project is handled by linguists who speak the language natively.

  1. 01

    Translation

    Documents, notices, forms, reports and websites, translated between English or Hindi and any language of Manipur, or between two of them.

  2. 02

    Transcription

    Accurate, time-stamped transcripts of interviews, meetings, focus groups and field recordings.

  3. 03

    Subtitles & captions

    Subtitles for films, documentaries, training videos and social media, delivered as SRT or VTT files.

  4. 04

    Proofreading & editing

    A careful second read of existing text for accuracy, spelling, grammar and tone before it goes public.

  5. 05

    Voiceover & recording

    Natural voiceovers by native speakers for awareness campaigns, e-learning, announcements and advertisements.

  6. 06

    Localization

    Apps, websites and campaigns adapted for Manipur's communities, including names, references and formats as well as words.

  7. 07

    Cultural consulting

    Advice on names, customs, idiom and sensitivities, so your message is received the way you intend.

  8. 08

    Script conversion

    Meiteilon text converted between Bengali script and Meetei Mayek, with consistent spelling throughout.

  9. 09

    Speech & text data

    Recording, transcription and annotation of speech and text for AI and research in low-resource languages.

How we work

Simple to start, careful to finish

  1. 01

    Share your brief

    Send your files or links and tell us the languages, audience and deadline.

  2. 02

    Get a fixed quote

    We reply with a clear price and delivery date before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Translate & review

    A native-speaker linguist translates, then a second linguist reviews against the source.

  4. 04

    Deliver & support

    Receive files in the format you need, with revisions if anything needs adjusting.

  • Native-speaker linguists

    People who grew up speaking the language they translate into.

  • Second-linguist review

    Every translation is checked by a second person before delivery.

  • Confidential by default

    Files are shared only with the people working on them. NDAs on request.

  • Consistent terminology

    Glossaries for recurring clients, so names and terms never drift.

EROL Lab

Language technology for Manipur

Our name is also our research agenda. Alongside client work, we're building open models and datasets so that speakers of Manipur's languages get the same quality of digital tools as speakers of the world's largest languages.

  1. E

    Encoder

    Representing Manipur's languages so machines can understand them.

  2. R

    Reconstruct

    Carrying meaning faithfully between languages and scripts.

  3. O

    Optimize

    Making tools efficient and practical to run.

  4. L

    Language

    Keeping every language of Manipur strong in a digital world.

About EROL

Rooted in Imphal, serving all of Manipur

EROL Manipuri Language Service was founded in Imphal in 2025. We believe everyone in Manipur deserves information in the language they understand best, whichever of the state's many languages that is.

We bring together native-speaker linguists from communities across the state, and treat every language with the same care: faithful to meaning, clear to read, and respectful of the people who speak it.

  • Every community, equally. We serve all of Manipur's communities and keep our work neutral.
  • Faithful to meaning. Names, numbers and nuance carried over accurately.
  • Clear to read. Writing that sounds natural to the people you want to reach.

Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Write to us. We reply quickly.

Which languages do you work in?

Meiteilon (Manipuri) and the languages of Manipur's hill communities, 33 in all and listed above, plus English and Hindi. If you need a language that isn't listed, ask us.

How much does a translation cost?

It depends on the language pair, the length and subject of the content, and your deadline. We send a fixed quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How long will my project take?

We agree a delivery date with you when we send the quote. Tell us if you have a firm deadline. Urgent work is often possible.

Can you translate between two languages of Manipur?

Yes, for example from Meiteilon into Tangkhul or Paite. Many public-information projects need several languages at once, and we can coordinate them together.

Will my documents be kept confidential?

Yes. Files are shared only with the linguists working on your project, and we're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement.

Which file formats do you accept?

Word, PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text, audio and video files, and subtitle formats such as SRT and VTT. If you use another format, let us know.
