01 Services

Everything your words need to be understood in Manipur

From a single certificate to a full film, we help you reach Manipuri-speaking audiences with clarity and authenticity.

Translation

Manipuri ⇄ English and other languages. Documents, reports, forms, websites and marketing copy, written to read naturally.

Transcription & Subtitling

Accurate Manipuri transcripts and time-coded subtitles for films, interviews, lectures and online video.

Proofreading & Editing

A careful review of existing Manipuri text for accuracy, grammar, tone and consistency before it goes public.

Cultural Consulting

Guidance on idiom, tradition and sensitivities, so your message lands the way you intend with audiences in Manipur.

Voiceover

Natural Manipuri voiceover for videos, advertisements, e-learning courses and public announcements.

Content Localization

Adapting apps, websites and campaigns for Manipuri speakers, including references, formats and script, not just the words.
