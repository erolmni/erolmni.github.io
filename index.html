02 Services

What we do

From a one-page notice to a full documentary, every project is handled by linguists who speak the language natively.

  • Translation

    Documents, notices, forms, reports and websites, between English or Hindi and any language of Manipur.

  • Transcription

    Accurate, time-stamped transcripts of interviews, meetings and field recordings.

  • Subtitles & captions

    Subtitles for films, documentaries, training videos and social media, as SRT or VTT files.

  • Voiceover

    Natural voiceovers by native speakers for campaigns, e-learning and announcements.

  • Localization

    Apps, websites and campaigns adapted for Manipur's communities, beyond the words.

  • Proofreading

    A careful second read for accuracy, spelling, grammar and tone before you publish.

Also: cultural consulting, script conversion, and speech & text data for AI.
