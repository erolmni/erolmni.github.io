Services

everything your words need, in every language.

Nine services, one standard: native-speaker linguists, a second review, and delivery in the format you need.

01 What we do

nine ways we help.

Each service works between English or Hindi and any of the 33 languages of Manipur, or between two languages of Manipur.

  1. 01

    Translation

    Documents, notices, forms, reports and websites, translated so they read naturally to the people you want to reach.

    • Government notices & forms
    • Reports & research
    • Websites & marketing
  2. 02

    Transcription

    Accurate, time-stamped transcripts of recordings, with speaker labels where you need them.

    • Interviews & oral histories
    • Meetings & focus groups
    • Field recordings
  3. 03

    Subtitles & captions

    Timed subtitles that are easy to read on screen, delivered as SRT or VTT files.

    • Films & documentaries
    • Training videos
    • Social media
  4. 04

    Voiceover & recording

    Natural voiceovers and audio recordings by native speakers, delivered as broadcast-ready files.

    • Awareness campaigns
    • E-learning
    • Radio & announcements
  5. 05

    Localization

    Apps, websites and campaigns adapted for Manipur's communities, including names, references and formats.

    • Websites
    • Mobile apps
    • Campaigns
  6. 06

    Proofreading & editing

    A careful second read of existing text for accuracy, spelling, grammar and tone before it goes public.

    • Existing translations
    • Publications
    • Signage & packaging
  7. 07

    Cultural consulting

    Advice on names, customs, idiom and sensitivities, so your message is received the way you intend.

    • Names & terminology
    • Community engagement
    • Sensitive topics
  8. 08

    Script conversion

    Meiteilon text converted between Bengali script and Meetei Mayek, with consistent spelling throughout.

    • Bengali script to Meetei Mayek
    • Meetei Mayek to Bengali script
    • Style & spelling checks
  9. 09

    Speech & text data

    Recording, transcription and annotation of speech and text for AI and research in low-resource languages.

    • Speech recordings
    • Annotation
    • Evaluation data

03 Quality

our promises.

The same standard on every project, whatever the language or the size of the job.

  • Native speakers

    Linguists who grew up speaking the language they translate into.

  • Second review

    Every translation is checked by a second linguist before delivery.

  • Confidential

    Files are shared only with the people working on them. NDAs on request.

  • Consistent terms

    Glossaries for recurring clients, so names and terms never drift.
